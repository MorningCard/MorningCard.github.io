Privacy policy

Sealed with Kindness

Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short: we ask for very little, we keep it on your phone, we only use it to write your cards, and we never sell it or use it for advertising.

Who we are

Sealed with Kindness is made by Interbrew Limited (company number 16361880), Unit 8 Redkiln Close, Horsham, RH13 5QL, England. We are responsible for your information under UK data protection law. You can contact us at MorningCard.app@gmail.com.

What we collect

You can open your first card without telling us anything. If you choose to add your details, we ask for:

  • your first name
  • your birthday (day and month only, never the year)
  • up to three things you love
  • one person or pet you care about, if you'd like to tell us
  • the time you'd like your card to arrive

The app also keeps the cards you've opened, so you can read them again and so you're never sent the same card twice.

We don't ask for your surname, address, phone number, email or age, and we don't collect your location or contacts. There are no adverts and no tracking in the app.

Where it's kept

Your details and cards are kept on your phone. If you have Android backup switched on, they are included in your phone's backup to your own Google account, so a new phone can carry on where you left off. We can't see that backup.

How your cards are written

To write each morning's card, the app sends our card-writing service your first name, your star sign (worked out from your birthday; your birthday itself is not sent), the things you love, the person or pet you named, and short notes on your past cards so that none is repeated.

The service runs on Google Cloud in London. It uses Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, to write the card, then sends it straight back to your phone. Our service doesn't store any of this. Anthropic processes it only to write your card and, under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its AI models.

If the service can't be reached, the app uses one of its own built-in cards instead, and nothing is sent.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details. We receive only what we need to know whether your subscription is active. We may use a subscription service (RevenueCat) to keep track of this; it receives a random ID and your subscription status, not your name or details.

Why we use your information

We use it only to provide the service you've asked for: writing and delivering your daily card. That is our legal basis under UK GDPR (performance of a contract). We never sell it, share it for marketing, or use it to show you adverts.

How long we keep it

Your information stays on your phone until you change it or delete the app. You can change your details at any time in My details. Uninstalling the app removes everything from your phone. To remove it from your Google backup too, delete the app's backup in your phone's Google settings.

Your rights

You have the right to see, correct or delete your information, and to object to how it's used. Because we don't keep your information ourselves, the quickest way is in the app or by deleting it, but you are always welcome to email us and we'll help.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Children

Sealed with Kindness is for adults. It is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for any important change, let you know in the app.
